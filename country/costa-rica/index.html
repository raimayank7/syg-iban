IBAN format of Costa Rica: 22 characters, starts with CR

An IBAN of Costa Rica is 22 characters long and begins with CR. Here is what each part means, a made-up example, and how to check one.

The parts with a name here are: Bank code, account number. The bank decides the bank code and the account number; you never have to work them out yourself. The layout code CR2!n4!n14!n is how the registry writes this: a number, then n for digits, a for capital letters or c for letters or digits.

How to check an IBAN of Costa Rica

  1. Count the characters without spaces. There must be exactly 22.
  2. The first two must be CR, and the next two must be digits.
  3. After those four, the rest must follow the layout: 4 digits, then 14 digits.
  4. Run the check-digit test. The IBAN checker does all four steps for you and shows which one fails.

Writing it down

On paper, an IBAN is written in groups of four characters with spaces, as in the example above, so that it is easier to read and compare. In online banking and in files it is entered without spaces. Both forms are the same IBAN. Letters are always capitals.

How it compares

At 22 characters, it sits between the shortest IBAN in the registry (Norway, 15 characters) and the longest (Russia, 33 characters). The same length is used by Bahrain, Bulgaria, Georgia, Germany, Ireland, Montenegro, Serbia, United Kingdom, each with its own layout. After the country code it has digits only.

Common questions

How many characters does an IBAN of Costa Rica have?

An IBAN of Costa Rica always has 22 characters: the letters CR, two check digits and 18 more characters for the bank and the account.

What does an IBAN of Costa Rica start with?

It starts with CR, the country code, followed by two check digits.

Is Costa Rica in SEPA?

No. Costa Rica uses IBAN but is not part of the Single Euro Payments Area.
